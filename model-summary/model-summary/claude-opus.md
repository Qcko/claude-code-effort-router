# Claude Opus

## Overview
Claude 3 Opus is the most capable model in the Claude family, designed for complex, multi-step reasoning and knowledge-intensive tasks.

## Strengths

- **Complex reasoning**: Excels at multi-step logical deduction, mathematical problem-solving, and deep analysis
- **Nuanced understanding**: Best at grasping subtlety, context, and implicit meanings in complex scenarios
- **Knowledge breadth**: Strong across multiple domains including science, history, culture, and specialized fields
- **Creative tasks**: Superior performance on writing, creative problem-solving, and novel synthesis
- **Code generation**: Excellent for complex algorithm design and multi-file refactoring
- **Analysis depth**: Can perform thorough analysis of large documents and datasets
- **Edge cases**: Handles unusual or ambiguous scenarios better than smaller models
- **Consistency**: More reliable for tasks requiring sustained logical chains over many steps

## Weaknesses

- **Speed**: Slowest inference time among Claude models
- **Cost**: Highest token cost per request
- **Overkill for simple tasks**: Wastes resources on straightforward queries
- **Latency-sensitive applications**: Not ideal for real-time interactive scenarios requiring sub-second response times

## Best Suited For

- **Complex problem-solving**: Multi-domain reasoning, research analysis, scientific questions
- **Code review & refactoring**: Reviewing complex codebases, suggesting architectural improvements
- **Strategic planning**: Long-term planning, stakeholder communication, complex project planning
- **Detailed content creation**: Long-form writing, comprehensive documentation, creative works
- **Knowledge synthesis**: Combining information across multiple domains
- **Math & logic**: Advanced problem-solving, proofs, complex calculations
- **Comprehensive analysis**: Deep dives into complex topics, literature analysis
- **Novel edge cases**: Scenarios not commonly encountered in training data

## Token Budget Guidance

Use when quality and correctness are worth the latency and cost trade-off. Not suitable for high-volume, real-time applications.
